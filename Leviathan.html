General Summary

Every Project Moon game has a webcomic that is at least somewhat related to the story of the game, acting as context to something or just the game in webcomic form(cough cough Wonderlab). Leviathan follows the story of Vergilius before he got hired to become part of the LCB as a guide, which is basically all the information we get on him. It follows his life when he was part of an office, with an event happening that impacts his life forever.

Prologue

In the first part of Leviathan, we see that it's taking place during Christmas eve and the other people in his office gave him supplies and a costume to act as Santa Claus for the children at an Orphanage that Vergilius really cares about. However, when Vergilius goes to the orphanage to deliver presents, he only finds the rubble of the orphanage caused by Tomerry, with a lot of dead orphans in the rubble. Tomerry is an amalgamation of two people, Tom and Merry, when they were combined and went insane during a Warp Train, which traps people inside pocket dimensions for thousands of years to give that stored time to T. Corp, ending with W. Corp cleaners fixing everyone on the bus and wiping everyone's memories, with the outside only seeing that it took 3 seconds to arrive to its destination. However, during Library of Ruina, Tomerry gets an invitation to the Library, allowing them to escape the train and stay in that combined form. After Vergilius processes this, Iori, who did this in search of her son, finds him and they begin fighting, ending with Vergilius exhausted and heavily injured before we come back to the present.

Main Story

After that flashback, we go back to the present and see Vergilius with the rest of the coworkers in his office at a bar when one of the child survivors of the orphanage, Garnet, who is now grown up, comes up to him and asks if Vergilius could meet up with him and another orphan survivor, Lapis. Even though Vergilius refuses at first, he eventually comes around to it after Garnet says Lapis has been abducted by the Ring, later finding out that many orphans are being experimented on, seeing how much they can "refract"(be split across multiple timelines/alternate universes). So, Garnet, Vergilius, and Vergilius's office team up to attempt to save Lapis. Attending a Ring auction as an excuse to infiltrate the Ring corridor. After fighting through some Ring members, they manage to find where the orphans, including Lapis, are being experimented on. However, when they try to release them, a Ring Maestro(Jumsoon), a high-ranking officer of the Ring, finds them and starts attacking them. While this is happening, it is revealed that a member from Vergilius's offcie ratted their plan out to the Ring and he pulls a lever which causes Garnet to refract as well. This causes Garnet to go through multiple mirror world identities before settling on the Black Silence identity, but before he casn attack Jumsoon, he succumbs to his grief and despair from these mirror worlds, turnng into a garnet-colored cocoon. Back to the fight, both Jumsoon and Vergilius get talked to by Carmen, trying to convince each of them to distort by trying to accept her deal, with Jumsoon accepting her deal and distorting, while Vergilius denies her help and attains E.G.O. as a result. After much more clashing, he manages to defeat Jumsoon after he distorted into an abnormality called Schadenfreude because of his vying for other peoples' attentions. He manages to save Lapis, but she has become a different person due to the refraction, being called Charon now. After all this, Faust enters with the LCA, another branch of Limbus Company, to recruit Vergilius to the LCB.
