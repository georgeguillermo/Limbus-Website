Main Characters

THE SEPHIROT

Malkuth

Malkuth is one of the Sephirot that works for the Facility in the upper layer, Asiyah, and is the head of Control Team. The player meets her on Day 1, after Angela's introduction. During work, she always tries to bring out the best of herself, giving her all so as to not disappoint Angela or the Manager(The player/X), as well explaining the rules in understandable but emphatic terms, unlike Angela. Despite this demeanor, Malkuth remains highly professional, and she is noted by Yesod to have never referred to the employees by name, but only by their identification number, since she joined the company. She also considers herself to be slow and forgetful at times. During her past life as a human, going by Elijah, she used to only record experiments related to Cogito under the supervision of Ayin, however, after many times of asking to be experimented on with Cogito, she decides to do it herself, but this leads to her body slowly breaking down, Ayin doing nothing as she begs him to put her out of her misery. She represents the "Willingness to stand up straight" after completing her meltdown.

Yesod

Yesod is one of the Sephirot that works for the Facility in the upper layer, Asiyah, and is the head of the Information Team. The player meets him on Day 6, after unlocking his first expansion of his Department. He believes that dwelling on emotions is a hindrance in this line of work, and quickly moves past the deaths of his employees. This cold behavior has earned him a reputation as a cruel and unfeeling Sephirah. However, in reality, Yesod is struck deeply by each life lost, and therefore works to prevent as many casualties as possible. He refuses to connect with his employees, believing that any emotional attachment will lead to preventable disasters. He makes sure his personal feelings cannot be seen, and at times, expresses the desire to be a truly emotionless machine whose judgement cannot be clouded. He used to work as a researcher for Lob Corp, going by the name Gabriel, but, after Elijah's death, he appeared unmoved, but began to wear more concealing clothing. However, he became rational to an obsessive degree, and eventually began scratching himself. After being forced into a medical examination, his scratching compulsion became worse, and he eventually scratched himself to death. He represents "The Rationality to Maintain Discretion".

Hod

Hod is one of the Sephirot that works for the Facility in the upper layer, Asiyah, and is the head of the Training Team. As Sephirah of the Training Team, she often worries about her employees, and appears to care for them deeply, even offering counseling for them to keep them sane. She is somewhat unsure of herself and responds well to validation from others. She wants to be acknowledged as a good Sephirah by her employees and the other Sephirah. She used to be a worker for Lob Corp, going by the name Michelle, being the youngest one out of them all. However, Michelle decided to reveal Lobotomy Corporation's experiments and plans to the Head, as a result of witnessing the horrible deaths of Elijah, Gabriel, Giovanni and Enoch. This led to an attack by the Head on the facility, causing many key employees' deaths. Hod admits that she was being a hypocrite and that she was only being compassionate to her employees for her own sake. However, Hod says she will continue to try to be a nice person if it can help or comfort somebody, even if people hate her. She represents "The Hope to be a Better Person".

Netzach

Netzach is one of the Sephirot that works for the Facility in the upper layer, Asiyah, and is the head of the Safety Team. Netzach strongly dislikes his job at the Corporation. He feels helpless and responsible for the death of his employees, and prefers escaping from these obligations by drinking beer and Enkephalin during work, wishing he would be scrapped or replaced. Netzach, who went by the name Giovanni in his past life, knew Carmen from a very young age, becoming childhood friends leading to him being interned as a patient of Carmen's company since an unknown time. After Carmen's death, Ayin takes advantage of him by saying that participating in the Cogito experiments could find a way to revive Carmen. The experiment performed on Giovanni was long, requiring multiple testing and each injection made Giovanni become more disconnected of reality, living in the lie that Carmen could be saved and expecting his noble sacrifice to be for her good. Ayin knew that they couldn't save her from the beginning but telling him about it will ruin his dream of saving her. The experiment ended with Giovanni's death. Netzach admits that he resented X(The player) from the beginning, but says that now he thinks they were sent to save him. Netzach has accepted to continue his life in the facility, representing "The Fearlessness to keep on Living".

Tiphereth A + B

The Tiphereths are two of the Sephirot that work for the facility in the middle layer, Briah, and are the heads of the Central Command Team. The one on the left is Tiphereth B while the one on the right is Tiphereth A. Tiphereth A is blunt and not afraid to speak her mind. She has high expectations for the manager and often criticizes the other Sephirot. She tends to expect bad results. However, Tiphereth B is more soft-spoken and kind compared to his sister, encouraging the manager and the other Sephirot instead of insulting them. He is optimistic and hopes things will get better in the facility. Despite him willing for a bright future, he admits that he had never enjoyed his life. Before becoming Sephirot, Tiphereth A was named Lisa and Tiphereth B was named Enoch, who both lived on the backstreets until they were taken into Lob Corp by Carmen. Lisa resembled her Tiphereth form, having long blond hair with a bow, but with purple eyes and an orange dress with tan shoes. Enoch wore a blue hoodie, with brown hair and blue eyes. During their first time in the facility, Lisa expresses discomfort, wanting to go home. Enoch says that this is their home now, and says that while there is no "mom", they could consider Carmen their new mom from now then. Through different cutscenes, it is revealed that Enoch has secretely been participating in experiments, and, it is later revealed that Enoch willingly gave himself up for the Corporation's Cogito experiments and died as a result. Lisa mumbles hateful things to Ayin and Carmen, saying that Carmen should have been the one to die instead of Enoch. iphereth A doesn't know what he wanted to obtain with his sacrifice, questioning if it was even worth it. She says she doesn't know, mentioning that she never expected anything, but she counts on the manager, believing that there was a reason for every sacrifice made and that eventually, everything will be okay. Tiphereth represents the "Expectation of the Meaning of Existence."

Gebura

Gebura is one of the Sephirot that works for the Facility in the middle layer, Briah, and is the head of the Disciplinary Team. Gebura has a tough and serious personality, considered more strict, aggressive or rude to most employees, even the Manager. She enforces the rules of the facility without mercy, having a great despise against cowards, employees who feel pity, and those who fear death. Along with that, she has the same disposition with Abnormalities, having a desire to make them suffer and never going 'soft' against them, as she knows the true nature of Abnormalities. Before Gebura was turned into a sephirot, she was the legendary color fixer named "The Red Mist", whose real name was Kali, and she protected her community in the backstreets. Her many battles won her the admiration of others, and soon the attention of Carmen as she searched for personnel for the company. She gave the opportunity to Kali, thinking that she could bargain with her for cheaper prices, but mostly because of her kindness for trying to repay an Old Lady's funeral for the food she had been given to her. With Kali applying for the company, Carmen told her to protect the employees of the facility as her task, with the warning that problems will soon come. When the facility was breached by the head, instead of running, she decided to fight the Head head on, taking down 2 Claws, but also suffering major wound due to it, losing an eye and an arm, leading to her fight with Garion. However, she was able to catch Garion off guard and dealt a fatal blow to her with the E.G.O. weapon "Mimicry" which was extracted from an ALEPH abno. Gebura was similar to Kali, but her uncontrollable anger led to many reboots as a sephirah, as she failed to protect the people that were put into her responsibility against the monsters of the facility and the subordinates of The Head, leading to her representing "The Courage to Protect".

Chesed

Chesed is one of the Sephirot that works for the Facility in the middle layer, Briah, and is the head of the Welfare Team. Chesed is sleepy and enjoys coffee, despite the lack of a need to sleep. He is very easygoing, and lax with the members of his team, finding pride in the fact that his team has the best approval rating. Despite this, he has a rather cynical attitude, seeming to have come to terms with their eventual deaths due to being used by Angela. Before becoming a sephirah, he was a worker in Lob Corp, going by the name Daniel, who was convinced by Carmen to leave his wing and join the team. However, one random day when he was working, a mysterious woman who breaks into the room and claims to be a member of the Head. After asking a random employee for Daniel's name, the figure requests that he open the sector contaning the abnos, adding that it would be easier to comply and allow some people to be saved rather than forcing her to take matters into her own hands, leading to all the abnos breaching and killing many employees. Daniel talks to Ayin through an intercom, seemingly about to die as well, filled with regret due to his choices, haunted by the dead bodies of his coworkers and perceiving himself as the cowardly traitor who sealed everyone's fate. While unsure whether he'll be able to face the fears that might come, in the end, Chesed decides to not run away anymore, so he represents "Those who are Faithful and Trustworthy".

Binah

Binah is one of the Sephirot that works for the Facility in the lower layer, Atziluth, and is the head of the Extraction Team. The player can meet her from Day 37 to 42, depending if the first expansion is unlocked. Binah has a somber and sadistic personality, showing no empathy with her lack of emotions and seeing herself as someone detached from humanity. She enjoys taunting with a condescending expression, adores pain and suffering as her source of satisfaction, and marks that what she does is only business, but all in a calm and eerie speech. Before being turned into a Sephirah, she was an arbiter of The Head, going by the name "Garion", being the arbiter who raided the facility along with multiple Claws. Shortly after a mission to handle an incident involving executives of H Corp., Garion had been assigned to wipe out the Outskirts lab, having been given intel from Michelle. She managed to wipe out nearly the entirety of the facility, but was mortally wounded by the Red Mist, her brain being used for intel about The Head and The City. Binah declares that she shall witness over every step he took, no matter how tiring or painful was the sight. She tells the Manager to simply proceed as he has always done, then he may just triumph, her character representing "The Eye Facing the Fear; Breaking the Cycle".

Hokma

Hokma is one of the Sephirot that works for the Facility in the bottom layer, Atziluth, and is the head of the Record Team. The player can meet him from Day 37 to 42, depending if the first expansion is unlocked. Hokma is a collected person with a solemn and prudent personality. He shows a clear knowledge of time and the cycles, as well how time affect lives in many ways. Hokma shows care for Ayin, explaining to him the work of time inside the facility while showing him that he wants to aid him while still trying to reach his goal. He is shown indifferent about the lives of others as he knows the regrets, the suffering and the fear, that inside there, are meaningless. Before Hokma was a Sephirah, he went by Benjamin and was previously a co-worker since the start of Lobotomy Corporation, being loyal to Ayin since the beginning and aiding him in his goals. Although he is a Sephirah, we don't know exactly how he got killed, since he survived the raid on the facility, but it's implied that Angela killed him after trying to warn the player, being thrown into the lower levels as a Sephirah. He represents "The Eye Embracing the Past; Building the Future" because he is both deeply tied to the weight of time and his past actions, but also looking toward the future by trying to help Ayin/X realize their goals.

THE NON-SEPHIROT

Angela

Angela is a highly advanced Artificial Intelligence whose roles include the surveillance of the entire facility, giving X advice on how to interact with Abnormalities and handle breaches, acting as the manager of the Sephirot, and various other tasks around the facility. At the start of the game, Angela's personality is happy and upbeat, saying things like "Good luck!" and giving the Player compliments such as "it means that you are doing good work". She still maintains this demeanor even under grotesque circumstances, such as when she pours a bottle of champagne to celebrate that the Player has killed an employee for the first time. However, she becomes more negative and even hostile towards X as more days go by. Angela was programmed and constructed over a non-specific time of "many years". According to her, "all sorts of new algorithms and functions were created and a new history of AI was written" due to her creation. The thing that sets Angela apart from other Artificial Intelligences is, apparently, her ability to "express emotions like most human beings". She also perceives time 100 times slower. During the true ending of Lobotomy Corporation, Angela says has seen everything, over and over again, and throughout all of it she was allowed no relief or respite. Her job as an AI was to do absolutely nothing but follow Ayin's orders, repeating a never-ending play with her at the sidelines; pulling every string and doing every job, yet never allowed a bow at the curtain call. A refused to acknowledge her until the very end. As such, Angela has come to the conclusion that she deserves more than this, and as such, will take her happy ending by force, unconcerned with who this will affect, leading to the Distortion phenomenon and the "White Nights and Dark Days" to happen.

Ayin

Ayin is the founder of Lobotomy Corporation and coworker of Carmen and the Sephirot. He focuses more on the result rather than the process itself, willing to sacrifice everything so long as the goal is fulfilled. He is quite stubborn due to this nature, causing him to take the situation to its extremes. That goal was given by Carmen as a legacy to fulfill rather than an objective created by himself, just because he felt the need to complete her will and what have they worked on for so long. Ayin, Carmen, and the other pre-sephirah worked together on the Seed of Light project, however, after it ended in the deaths of many people, and Ayin's inability to supervise Carmen, this led to Carmen committing suicide, but, after Ayin found her dead body, he removed Carmen's nervous system from her body and placed it in a reservoir where she began to produce Cogito for continuous experimentation and become the "The Well", where the substance is drawn out from. Even with her nervous system as the only remaining part of her body, Carmen still lives on in the chamber, though she cannot see, hear, talk, or otherwise communicate with those that remain. We later find out that we are playing as a version of Ayin who wiped his memory for the sake of the Seed of Light simulation to work.

Carmen

Carmen was the head researcher of The Seed of Light project, the main catalyst of the unfolding events at the Cogito Research Facility, the Lobotomy Corporation Wing, and eventually the entire City. Carmen was a woman animated by a unique drive and personal way of seeing the world. Unlike most denizens of The City, who are often nihilistic and corrupt, Carmen stood out as a highly enthusiastic, altruistic, and ambitious person, guided by a desire to change the City's status quo and help its denizens out of their misery. She was perceptive and charismatic, easily predisposing people to herself and knowing how to quickly gain their trust. Carmen highly valued human life, and her goal was to bring the end of all suffering in the City by curing its people, as she believed them to be affected by a "disease of the mind", which curbed a person's will and left them unable to face adversity. Aware of the significance of this plan, Carmen was willing to make great sacrifices for the cause, and she would struggle when faced with failure. After Enoch's death, Carmen fell into a state of depression that shook her beliefs and faith in what she'd been working for, as the cost of her failure, which had led to the death of a child, outweighed any positive outcome of the experiment. She remained in this state until her suicide attempt. However, we learn that she is the voice that calls out to people during times of extreme emotional distress, trying to make them distort.
