THE SINNERS

Yi Sang

Yi Sang is Sinner #1 in Limbus, but we don't know if he was actually the first sinner to be recruited by Limbus Company chronologically. He is one of the most important sinners because he is the one who invented/discovered mirror worlds with his involvement in "The League of Nine Littérateurs", which was a group of researchers and scientists in District 19 who focused on technology, but the group split after one member, Dongrang, snitched to T corp. about it, which led to everyone going their seperate ways. He is based on the real life poet by the same name, which just so happens to translate to "ideal", which is the set up for some jokes.

Faust

Faust is Sinner #2 in Limbus. She is very interesting because she was the person to recruit most of the sinners and even a color fixer to be the "guide"(Vergilius). Furthermore, she is the one who built the bus that they travel on, Mephistopheles, which is powered by eating the enkephalin found in the brain, which means they shove the bodies of whoever they kill into Mephi. Additionally, she has access to basically a discord server inside her head called the "Gesellschaft", which is a collection of Fausts from every universe that can give information, but she also has to contribute in order to access this information. This leads to a conflict in the (current) late game of Infernum, where she gets named Stupid Faust by the Gesellschaft and gets prohibited from getting any more information from it. Her character comes from the book from the same name as her, Faust.

Don Quixote

Don Quixote is Sinner #3 in Limbus. She is interesting because of her actual origin and her shoes, Rocinante. Don Quixote is a second-generation bloodfiend, which are like vampires(only in the fact that they both drink blood, everything else is different) but with blood related powers, and the fact that they have to drink blood or else they go crazy. The only reason she isn't going crazy for blood is because of her shoes, Rocinante, which makes her forget everything about her past, but after her canto, she's basically roleplaying as Don Quixote after returning to her original self. If she takes off her shoes, her urges stop being inhibited and she goes hungry for blood, almost wiping out the sinners twice because of this, with the second time needing Vergilius to stop her. She is based on Miguel de Cervantes's "Don Quixote de La Mancha".

Ryoshu

Ryoshu is Sinner #4 in Limbus. Even though it isn't brought up until it is almost time for her canto, she has a deep relationship with all the finger-syndicates(Pinky, Ring, Middle, Index, and Thumb). Syndicates are basically crime groups throughout the city, with lesser syndicated often being a branch of a bigger syndicate, for example, the kurokumo clan is a subsidiary of the Thumb and therefore has to abide by all its rules. The reason she is deeply related to all of them is because of a project called "House of Spiders", where she was born for the sole reason of learning how to use a specific relic that can erase anything it slices, at the cost of her memory. Furthermore, Ryoshu isn't her real name, its the name her adopted(I think) child, Araya, gave her when trying to pronounce her actual name, Yoshihide. She is based on the protagonist from Hell Screen by Ryūnosuke Akutagawa.

Mersault

I think you already get the pattern but Mersault is Sinner #5 in Limbus. He is unique because before his canto dropped, we barely knew anything about his apart from the fact that he might've had a connection to N corp. Furthermore, he kinda has a strange personality, never having any judgement on anything and not doing anything until Dante, the manager, tells him to, not out of laziness but out of not wanting to judge. This is thoroughly explored and overcome during his canto, where he has to face his past and his fears of the gaze of other(which appears to be a genetic thing/j). He is based off of Mersault from Albert Camus's "The Stranger".

Hong Lu

Hong Lu is Sinner #6. If you are very attentive to the game, you'll be able to guess what's special about him, but it's only outright stated to us during his canto that his jade-colored eye, which is an actual jade, is used so that the elders of Hongyuan, his home, could watch the outside world through his eyes, not being able to leave the confines of a sealed room in Hongyuan because of the things they did to their bodies in the search for immortality. His whole canto is about him facing his long-suppressed feeling and standing up against the elders, even having to face his own grand-mother, who fuses with the golden bough, which is basically a branch created by the Lobotomy Corp, but eventually being able to come out on top using his E.G.O.. His character is based on a book called The Dream of the Red Chamber.

Heathcliff

Heathcliff is Sinner #7. He is pretty regular compared to most of the LCB, Limbus Company Bus department, apart from being british. He was raised in the Earnshaw manor after Mr. Earnshaw was walking through the District 20 backstreets, saw young Heathcliff, and decided to raise him in the District 20 nest along his Mr. Earnshaw two children, Hindley and Catherine. However, the turning point comes when Mr. Earnshaw dies and Hindley is put in charge of the estate, ordering the head butler, Nelly, to neglect Heathcliff, but Heathcliff decides to stay because of Catherine. However, after eavesdropping in a conversation between Nelly and Cathy, where Cathy says that she won't/can't marry Heathcliff, he leaves because he sees living in the manor as destroying her. On the other hand, it is only clarified that, when Heathcliff comes back years later with the LCB, Cathy couldn't marry him because of his social status and Hindley always degrading him. His character is based off Heathcliff from Wuthering Heights.

Ishmael

Ishmael is Sinner #8. She used to be a sailor who traveled through the Great Lake when she was younger, persuaded by the captain of the Pequod, Ahab, to try to hunt for a pallid whale, which is like a gigantic whale swimming in the Great Lake. This gave her experinece about the strict rules of the Great Lake while also giving her a deep hatred for Ahab because she basically sacrificed the entire Pequod, crew included, to try to kill the pallid whale, which failed, with Ishmael being the only person to have escaped being eaten by the whale. Also this canto is when my goat Ricardo is introduced. She is based on the protagonist from "Moby Dick".

Rodion

Rodion, also known as Rodya, is Sinner #9. Before joining the LCB, she lived in the backstreets of District 25, ruled by Y. corp. She used to be part of a group of Robin Hoods called "Yurodiviye", who aimed to redistribute wealth to prevent the concentration of wealth. However, after witnessing the death of a young member of the group by starvation and seeing that Sonya, the leader of the group, is unbothered by this, she decides that the group had strayed too far from their original goal, leading to her taking matters into her own hands as she decides to murder a tax collector. However, this gets everyone in the village in deep trouble because that tax collector just so happened to be a member of the middle syndicate, leading to them taking rveneg by killing a lot of the people in the village, making Rodion feel a massive amount of guilt that she still carries, because her canto is a unique one that didn't resolve her inner conflict. This is also shown through the fact that everyone who has had a canto so far has also had a "bad end" identity, which shows what they would have ended up like if they went down the worst road they could have, however, Rodion doesn't have one, meaning that her regular LCB identity is her "bad end" identity. She is based off of Fyodor Dostoyevsky's "Crime and Punishment".

Sinclair

Sinclair is Sinner #11. If you're wondering why he isn't sinner #10, it's because Dante was Sinner #10 until he distorted, which led to a clock head prosthetic replacing his head, which made him unable to fight, leading to him turning into the manager of the Sinners. Unlike most Sinners, Sinclair was born in the nest of K corp, living inside the town that was the main producer of prosthetics in K Corp. However, when his family secured a contract with P corp to get full body prosthetics later, Sinclair was opposed to the idea, but never voiced it to his family. Kromer, one of his classmates and avid prosthetic hater, took advantage of this one day by getting him to give her the key to his basement, where they would sneak into a vent into L. corp and see an abnormality before Sinclair left out of fear. At the same time, Sinclair was also noticing Demian, another one of his classmates who happened to have a mark on his forehead. Sinclair planned to confess to his family that he stole the basement key, but before he could, he found them being killed by Kromer and another armored man. Kromer orders the man to bring Sinclair to safety, but he wakes up in an alleyway where Demian talk to him about overcoming his fears. After this, Sinclair joined the LCB. The characters in his canto are based on "Demian" by Herman Hesse.

Outis

Outis is sinner #12. We don't know much about Outis apart from the fact that she participated in the first smoke war, with a second smoke war foreshadowed to happen in the future of Limbus, which is the same war that Gregor was a popular participant in. This is when she got most of her commanding experience that she shows through her actions in the LCB. Outis is also greek for "Nobody", which just so happens to be the nickname that Oddyseus uses in "The Oddysey". Sorry for the short description, more will be posted when her canto releases. :(

Gregor

Gregor is sinner #13. Similar to Outis, he also participated in the first smoke war, being the poster boy for G. corp to experiment on soldiers to give them bug-like mutations, with Gregor's bug arm being one of the best case scenarios for this. Even though he encourage many people to join the war and many soldiers had bug mutations to make them stronger, G. corp still lost the smoke war, leading to their collpase as a corporation and the collapse of the wing. His mother, Hermann, is also the leader of the League of Nine Littérateurs, and also was the one to "gift" him with abilities whose full capabilities we are yet to see(fortunately). However, we see in Canto 9 the first part of Hermann's "gift" as he begins his transformation into the Roach Emperor, gaining power that could challenge Vergilius and barely being able to incapacitate him in order to stop the transformation before it's too late. The transformation was caused by a combination of stress and PTSD occuring at the same time

THE LCB (NON SINNER)

Vergilius

Vergilius acts as the "guide" for the Sinners in Limbus Company, only being able to get involved when it is absolutely necessary for the survival of Dante or the success of the mission. He is a color fixer, under the name "The Red Gaze", who has had a long history in the city and the horrors it can bring. In Leviathan, the webcomic released before Limbus that acts as context to Vergilius and his backstory, learning how and why he earned E.G.O.. He wields a relic that is a gladius sword that also heats up to burn people. We will learn more about him in Leviathan. He is based on "The Divine Comedy" by Dante Aleghieri.

Charon

Charon is the driver of Mephistopheles and just acts really silly in general. However, we do not get to learn who she is inside of Limbus, we only get to know her during the events of Leviathan. If you want to get a summary of who she is, she used to be names Lapis, who was one of the only survivors of an attack Iori, another color fixer called "The Purple Tear", did on an orphanage that Vergilius had to stop. However, after being experimented on by the Ring syndicate, she became a different person, turning into Charon.

Dante

Dantehhh used to be one of the 13 sinners part of the LCB, as we learn in the prologue Canto "Selva Oscura", but during the events of Selva Oscura, they begin to distort, needing to have their head replaced by a clock head prosthetic in order to stop it, at the cost of forgetting all of his past memories. He now acts as the manager of all the sinners in the LCB, also being able to "rewind" them if they get injured, basically healing them, due to his clock head prosthetic. However, we learn that this also causes his a lot of pain every time he does it.
