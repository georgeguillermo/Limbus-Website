STORY

Lobotomy Corporation is the first game by project moon and it basically sets up every main plot point or idea that's included in future games. It is basically the foundation of knowledge in the Project Moon Universe. This game is basically a management simulator where the thing you're trying to manage is your own sanity as you try to tough your way through this game. For all the hate I'm about to give this game, I still love it a lot because it is still fun in the end, even if what I say may not make it seem like it is. The main characters in this game are the Sephirah: Malkuth(Elijah), Hod(Michelle), Yesod(Gabriel), Netzach(Giovanni), Tiphereth A(Lisa) and B(Enoch), Gebura(Kali), Chesed(Daniel), Binah(Garion), Hokma(Benjamin), Angela, Carmen, Ayin, and X(The character you play as), with the names in parentheses being the names of the characters before they got turned into sephirah. This entire game is basically a simulation for the "seed of light", which was intended to heal the mental troubles of everyone in The City, with especially strong willed individuals being able to resonate with it during extreme moments of stress, forming the basis of E.G.O. (Extermination of Geometrical Organ). Before everyone got turned into robots, they were actual workers for Lobotomy corporation alongside Ayin, Carmen, and Angela. They were all convinced to work for Lob Corp because of the bright future that Carmen promised that she would bring through her experiments, giving speeches in public that was able to sway even the Red Mist to join, who was a very prominent color fixer. However, after a series of failed experiments related to cogito, which is, as Binah so aptly put it, like drawing liquid manifestations of a person's idea from a well, being their subconscious, caused the deaths of Elijah and Enoch, this caused Carmen to kill herself due to a deep depression and eventually led to the deaths of Gabriel, Giovanni, and Michelle as an aftereffect of those deaths. However, before Michelle died, she snitched to the head about the experiments and taboos happening there, which causes an arbiter of the Head, Garion, to come and kill almost everyone who was left in Lobotomy Corporation, with the exception of Ayin and Angela. which led to her feeling the guilt of causing a lot of death, leading to her suicide. However, when Garion was unleashing all the abnormalities and going through the facility, she encountered a heavily wounded Kali, who managed to kill Garion after catching her off guard with E.G.O., leading her Garion turning into the Sephirah Binah.

GAME OVERVIEW

Game Mechanics

This game is split into 50 days, with each day getting exponentially harder, especially if you don't have a wikipedia to help you throughout your day. Every run starts off with the abno "One Sin and Hundreds of Good Deeds", which is a representation of Christ(The Antichrist will be an abno later in the game) and it is basically a tutorial abno to train your nuggets, which are the tiny little workers you manage and send to work on abnos. There are 4 different work types you can do with abnormalities (Technically 5 but only 4 are available at a time): Repression, Instinct, Insight, and Attachment, each of which having different levels of success and even different effects on the nugget or facility for certain abnos. After most day, you get to choose from a selection from 3 abnormalities, to which you only know the designation number to if you haven't discovered them yet in a previous run. The difficulty of taking care of an abno ranges from ZAYIN, TETH, HE, WAW, and ALEPH in order of difficulty, with more difficult abnos giving more energy, but also having the risk to destroy your entire run if you don't manage it correctly. To stop you from farming low level abnos all day, the qliphoth gauge exists to level it out. The qliphoth guage raises by one bar every time you complete work on an abno, causing an ordeal after the bar fills up, the severity ranging from dawn, noon, dusk, and midnight, and the different types of ordeals being: white, amber, crimson, violet, indigo, and green. However, white ordeals only happen during the last 4-5 days, replacing the regular ordeals. At the same time, these ordeals also cause qliphoth meltdowns to occur with random abnos, forcing you to work with those abnos or else they'll breach. At the beginning of the game, you only start off with Malkuth's floor, the control team, and slowly unlock lower floors as you progress, ony being able to unlock the next floor once you've filled all the teams in the previous floor with abnos. After each day, you get a certain amount of lob points depending on how successfully you completed the day, which can let you get more nuggets and upgrade the aspects of nuggets that correspond to the 4 different types of work. Before each day, you can choose which nuggets go to which department and what weapons are armor you can give them. E.G.O. weapons and armor come from working with a abno enough to where you get enough points to fully unlock all knowledge about it, being able to extract weapons and armor from it if you have enough points to extract it. Later in the game, you can have the option to go through a sephirah meltdown, which hinders an aspect of your game for the day until you beat the meltdown, only allowing you to progress once you've reached a certain qliphoth meltdown level and extracted all the energy for the day. After beating the day, you get a buff for your facility and nuggets along with getting lore as to the sephirah's life before being turned into one.

Music and Visuals

Hokma Suppression Music (My favorite)

Unlike the other 2 Project Moon games, Lobotomy Corp doesn't have as much music going on, only having music for each sephirah meltdown, each trumpet warning, and regular music for when neither is applicable. The first trumpet acts as more of a warning to deal with whatever might've broken out. However, the second trumpet is a more urgent sound, telling you that if you don't take care of the threat immediately, you will probably lose the day. On the other hand, the third trumpet acts as a sign of hopelessness in your daym signalling that you probably won't be able to recontain whatever broke out, meaning you either have to restart the day or focus on doing nothing but recontaining the abno.
