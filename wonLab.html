Summary

Although it originally was canon and referenced sometimes, it became not canon after the creator behind it asked for it to be removed, but I'm still gonna summarize it because it was once canon. This webcomic follows the story of Catt, Rose, and Taii as they work for branch of Lobotomy Corporation. This comic also introduces many ideas that are brought up later during Limbus Company, like abberations of abnos, and E.G.O. corrosion, which are core aspects of the story. It starts with this branch suffering a breach of Hookah Caterpillar, a WAW abnormality capable of dealing Pale damage (essentially capable of manipulating life force). The Disciplinary Team is wiped out, except for Catt and Narae, and a group of agents arrive to replace the deceased agents, with Catt attacking them, causing all of them except Rose to run away. Narae, after being traumatized, distorts and has to be killed by Catt and Rose. Rose is a Nest resident who discovered the truth about the City; the class division in the City, and how disturbing and cruel the City is to its citizens. Rose vows to change the Wing, the corporation, to change the City. Taii is a flowery person who likes cute things and starts off with a weak disposition to the horrors. As time goes on, they begin to become stern and protective of the people they care about. Catt is revealed to be similar to Taii, but instead of becoming protective, they found that the only way to survive in this hell of a company is to toss away their emotions and to beat and/or kill everything (even co-workers) that would endanger Catt. Rose dies shortly after to an abberation, and it causes everything to go wrong, Catt distorts, every abno gets released, White Night and Dark Days occurs, and the manager of the branch also distorts. Taii manages to undistort Catt for a short while before they distort again, but this time, they create a portal and lead to abnormalities somewhere else, leaving Taii alone.
