General Summary

This webnovel is the one that sets up the context around Library of Ruina and the Distortion Phenomenon caused after the "White Night and Dark Days" event in the middle of District 12. As this is a novel compared to the other two being webcomics, Distortion Detective is MUCH longer, having around 43 chapters, which is very high compared to Leviathan and Wonderlab. Since this novel is so long, I will try to condense it into a much shorter summary instead of focusing on everything they do, only focusing on major events. This novel follows the story of Moses, a detective, and Ezra, her assistant, as they solve distortion cases all around District 14, trying to uncover the truth as to what happened, while also trying to face the psychological struggles that she suffers through due to her work. Another important character is YuRia, who is the manager/owner of her own workshop that helps Moses and Ezra upgrade their equipment after helping YuRia on a case. Another important character is Vespa, who was an N. corp taboo hunter, and he hunts down YuRia because she has recorded footage made in N. corp (which is taboo there), however, he is trapped inside YuRia's atelier, with the only food source he had being Moses's arm, which he cut off earlier. He is trapped there for 2 weeks, which elads to him getting fired from N. corp, so he reluctantly joines Moses's offices because Dias, the executive manager of the Udjat, has a history with Moses, which could help him restore his employment. However, we learn that Dias may have some darker motives than what appears on the surface, having been the cause of two different mass-distortion incidents in two different cities. The last major story line that began before the novel got cancelled was an incident at Hotel Larrière, which is managed by a bloodfiend, where the staff of the hotel need the help of Moses's office in order to stop a rogue bloodfiend.
